Writing is where unfinished ideas are tested in public. This page intentionally does not reproduce article titles, dates, or reading-time statistics — those would imply a level of verification not performed. Read the articles on DEV.to, on their own terms.

What you will and won't find here

  • You will find: a single, honest link to the DEV.to profile, and a description of what the writing is for.
  • You won't find: a fabricated list of article titles, fabricated publication dates, fabricated reading-time statistics, fabricated view counts, or any claim about peer review.
  • You won't find: a claim that publishing on this site improves AI citation probability, search ranking, or any other measurable outcome. There is no validated measurement supporting such a claim.

What the writing is for

The writing has two functions. First, it forces an unfinished idea into sentences — the cheapest way to discover that an idea is not yet ready. Second, it leaves a public trail of when an idea was revised and why, which is harder to maintain inside a repository alone.

If a specific article becomes central to one of the research directions, it may be linked directly from the relevant project page rather than here. That decision will be made per-article, and only after reading it.
